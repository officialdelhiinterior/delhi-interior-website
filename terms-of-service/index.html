Terms of Service.

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Delhi Interior. These Terms of Service govern your relationship with Delhi Interior regarding architectural design, modular woodwork manufacturing, and turnkey home execution services.

1. Scope of Work & Itemized Quotation

All work undertaken by Delhi Interior is strictly defined in a signed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and 3D design agreement. Any client-requested alterations or additions after sign-off will be quoted separately in writing before commencement. We commit to transparent, itemized pricing with zero hidden surcharges.

2. Payment Milestone Schedule

To ensure steady procurement of high-grade raw materials and uninterrupted manufacturing, projects follow a transparent milestone schedule:

  • Booking Advance (10%): Site measurement, space planning, and 3D architectural rendering.
  • Design Sign-Off (40%): Material finalization, material procurement, and workshop carcass fabrication.
  • On-Site Civil & Framing Completion (40%): Civil masonry, false ceiling, and modular assembly on site.
  • Zero-Snag Handover (10%): Final quality audit, snag fix, deep cleaning, and key handover.

3. Quality Standards & Material Specifications Policy

Delhi Interior builds exclusively with genuine, certified materials including IS:710 boiling waterproof marine plywood, Action TESA HDMR boards, and authentic hardware from Blum, Hettich, and Hafele. Manufacturer test certificates and authenticity records are provided upon handover.

Care Guidelines: To preserve finish longevity, clients receive clear maintenance guidelines covering moisture protection, hardware care, and surface cleaning protocols.

4. Project Timelines & Stage Milestones

We operate on agreed project schedules structured around clear stage milestones (civil preparation, workshop fabrication, on-site joinery, and finishing). Timelines commence after architectural drawing sign-off and site readiness.

5. Dispute Resolution & Jurisdiction

Any disputes arising out of or related to our services will first be addressed through mutual good-faith mediation. If unresolved, the dispute shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the civil courts in New Delhi, India.
