Sleek Architecture

Light Flow Without Compromise. Acoustic Privacy with Minimal Sightlines.

Contemporary luxury homes and modern executive offices demand open visual flow without sacrificing acoustic privacy or spatial zoning. Traditional wooden partitions can feel heavy and block natural daylight, while low-grade commercial aluminium sections rattle and warp under everyday use.

At Delhi Interior, our specialized architectural metal and glazing team engineers ultra-slim profile systems in our Mohammadpur workshop. Using precision miter joinery, high-tensile concealed soft-close dampers, and certified Saint-Gobain toughened safety glass, we build partitions that glide effortlessly and endure decades of rigorous urban living.

Slim Sightlines (16–25mm) Minimalist profile frames maximizing glass surface area and daylight.
Acoustic DGU Glass Double-glazed sound attenuation up to 42 dB for quiet home studies.

Workflow

Our 5-Stage Engineering Journey

From millimeter laser survey to final silicone sealing, executed with surgical discipline.

01

Laser Survey

Precision electronic laser measurement verifying wall plumb, floor level, and ceiling deflection.

02

3D CAD Detailing

Detailed cross-section drawings showing profile intersections, glass cutouts, and roller anchor points.

03

Workshop Machining

CNC profile cutting, precision 45-degree miter corner joints, and Saint-Gobain glass edge polishing.

04

Quality Trial

Full pre-assembly and soft-close mechanism calibration in our South Delhi workshop before delivery.

05

Clean Installation

Fast, dust-free mechanical fixing on-site, optical laser alignment, and neutral-cure silicone perimeter sealing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about architectural aluminium and glass systems.

We exclusively use 6063-T6 architectural-grade virgin aluminium extrusion alloy. It provides superior structural rigidity for slim sightlines (16mm to 25mm), perfect miter corner joints, and excellent anodized or electrostatic powder-coated surface durability.
We install 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm certified toughened safety glass from Saint-Gobain and AIS. For specialized requirements, we provide fluted/moru glass, acoustic laminated double glazing (DGU), tinted bronze/grey glass, and lacquered decorative glass.
Yes. Our acoustic aluminium glass partitions utilize double-glazed units (DGU) with PVB acoustic lamination and perimeter EPDM compression gaskets, achieving noise reduction levels between 36 dB and 42 dB for quiet executive work in urban Delhi NCR.
Yes, we specialize in heavy-duty top-hung sliding systems with zero floor tracks. This ensures uninterrupted Italian marble or wooden flooring between living and dining areas while heavy-duty concealed dampers deliver silent, finger-touch operation.
Every shower enclosure is custom-measured on-site with millimeter laser precision. We fabricate frameless and semi-frameless cubicles with 10mm toughened glass, solid SS304/brass hinges, magnetic anti-splash seals, and hydrophobic anti-limescale coating.
All profile cutting, miter machining, and glass fabrication are pre-engineered in our South Delhi workshop. On-site work is limited to clean mechanical assembly, precision laser leveling, and perimeter sealing, minimizing on-site dust and noise.
