Interior Design Cost in Delhi (2026): 1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK & 4BHK Price Guide per Sq Ft.

D
By Delhi Interior Editorial Team

Whether you just received the keys to a new builder floor in Greater Kailash, an expansive high-rise apartment on Dwarka Expressway, or are remodeling an established family home in South Delhi, one question immediately dominates every homeowner's mind: How much will interior design in Delhi actually cost?

If you have already started speaking with contractors, freelance designers, or corporate aggregator firms, you have likely encountered wild, contradictory estimates: one agency quotes ₹6 Lakh, another quotes ₹18 Lakh for the identical 3BHK carpet area, while luxury studios quote upwards of ₹35 Lakh. Why does interior pricing vary so drastically in Delhi NCR? And what are the real, per-square-foot benchmarks you should plan for in 2026?

At Delhi Interior, we operate our own woodworking workshop in Mohammadpur, South Delhi, executing turnkey residences without middleman markups. In this transparent 2026 pricing authority guide, our senior architects and cost engineers break down actual per-square-foot market rates, full 1BHK to 4BHK budget tiers, room-by-room expenses, and hidden contractor fees so you can plan your renovation with complete financial certainty.

4. How Delhi Housing Types Impact Your Renovation Costs

A cost estimate that works for a high-rise society in Sector 65 Gurugram will not match an independent builder floor in Defence Colony or a DDA flat in Vasant Kunj. Understanding your property's structural conditions is vital:

A. Gated Societies & High-Rise Condominiums (DLF, Godrej, Tata, Supertech)

High-rise apartments are handed over with basic civil finishes (vitrified tiles, electrical conduit layout, basic plumbing). However, strict Society RWA guidelines limit loud work to 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM and ban noisy drilling on weekends. Using an interior firm with an off-site modular workshop (like Delhi Interior) ensures 80% of woodworking is pre-fabricated off-site, avoiding society noise penalties and speeding up on-site assembly.

B. Independent Builder Floors (South Delhi, West Delhi, Gurugram)

Builder floors often boast grand 10.5-foot to 11.5-foot ceiling heights, expansive balconies, and bespoke floor plans. However, they require extensive custom woodwork: oversized teak pivot main doors, customized balcony aluminium glass railings, and deep wardrobe lofts that mass-market catalog companies cannot build.

C. DDA Flats & Heritage Resale Renovations

Older properties frequently conceal underlying civil issues: peeling wall plaster from dampness (seelan), outdated aluminum electrical wiring, and leaking bathroom drainage lines. Before starting furniture design, you must allocate ₹1.5 Lakh to ₹3.0 Lakh for civil waterproofing, chemical damp-barrier injections, and sanitary replacement.

5. The 4 Hidden Costs That Derail Unprepared Homeowners

If an interior quotation appears unusually low, be wary. Shady contractors routinely leave out essential work items, demanding extra payments halfway through the project:

  • 1. Civil Debris (Malba) Disposal: In Delhi municipal zones (MCD), dumping demolition debris in community garbage bins attracts severe penalties. Proper tractor transportation to authorized recycling sites costs ₹8,000 to ₹18,000 per apartment.
  • 2. Electrical Shifting & Cable Upgrades: Shifting AC points, embedding television conduit boxes, and running 4mm fire-proof copper cables for heavy microwave or geyser loads can add ₹25,000 to ₹50,000 if not included in your base quote.
  • 3. Goods and Services Tax (GST): Legitimate corporate studios and material suppliers charge 18% GST. Always confirm whether an estimate is pre-tax or post-tax before comparing quotes.
  • 4. Counterfeit Hardware Substitutions: Local contractors often substitute genuine German Hettich or Blum drawer runners for cheap unbranded copies stamped with fake logos, saving themselves ₹1,200 per drawer while ruining your kitchen within 18 months.

Frequently Asked Questions About Interior Design Costs in Delhi
