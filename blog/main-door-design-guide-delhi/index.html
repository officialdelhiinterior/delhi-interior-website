Main Door Design & Custom Wooden Doors in Delhi (2026): Types, Materials & Cost Guide.

D
By Delhi Interior Editorial Team
· 📅 November 21, 2026 · ⏱️ 10 min read

In Indian architecture and home design, the main door is far more than an entryway. It is the architectural anchor of your residence, the threshold where guests form their first impression, and according to Vastu Shastra, the primary conduit through which cosmic energy flows into your living space. Yet, when homeowners in South Delhi, Gurugram, or West Delhi embark on building or renovating their flat or kothi, they often struggle with a confusing array of options: Should you choose solid Burma Teak, a veneered flush door, or an oversized pivot door? How do you prevent doors from jamming during humid Delhi monsoons? And what is the realistic cost of custom door fabrication in Delhi NCR?

At Delhi Interior, we manufacture bespoke doors directly in our South Delhi workshop in Mohammadpur. In this comprehensive 2026 guide, our senior woodworkers and architects break down every critical facet of custom door making: popular designs, timber selection, hardwood chaukhats (door frames), smart biometric hardware, and an honest itemized price breakdown.

1. Why Custom Door Making Demands Specialized Engineering in Delhi

Delhi NCR experiences one of the world's harshest seasonal climate swings: scorching summer temperatures exceeding 45°C with near-zero humidity, followed by torrential monsoon downpours where relative humidity spikes above 85%, ending in chilly winter fogs. Cheap, mass-produced commercial doors react violently to these extreme fluctuations:

  • Thermal Expansion & Jamming: Unseasoned wood absorbs atmospheric moisture, expanding by several millimeters. Result: doors jam against their frames every July and August.
  • Sagging Due to Inadequate Hinges: Solid wood doors easily weigh between 60kg and 160kg. Mounting a heavy door on standard butt hinges inevitably leads to sagging, scraping against expensive Italian marble or vitrified flooring.
  • Surface Warping & Veneer Delamination: Using uncalibrated core boards or solvent-based adhesives causes surface veneers to bubble and bend within two seasons.

Solving these issues requires seasoned timber kiln-dried to under 12% moisture content, precision mortise-and-tenon jointed frames, and engineered hardware rated for high dynamic loads.

2. Top 5 Modern Main Door Design Styles in Delhi NCR

Depending on whether you reside in a modern high-rise apartment in Gurugram, an independent builder floor in Greater Kailash, or a sprawling villa in Vasant Vihar, here are the dominant door styles favored by Delhi's discerning homeowners:

A. The Grand Teak Pivot Entrance Door

The undisputed crown jewel of contemporary luxury. Rather than hinging from the side frame, a pivot door rotates on heavy-duty top and bottom hydraulic bearings set 4 to 8 inches inward from the wall. This allows for extraordinary dimensions—often 4.5 to 6 feet wide and up to 9 feet tall—moving effortlessly with the gentle touch of a finger. Features vertical CNC fluted grooves, a continuous floor-to-ceiling brass pull handle, and integrated biometric access.

B. Designer Flush Doors with Natural Wood Veneer & Metallic Inlays

The most popular choice for modern Delhi apartments. Built on an engineered solid core (such as HDHMR or seasoned pine blockboard) and pressed with exotic natural wood veneers—Smoked Oak, American Walnut, or Santos Rosewood. Subtle brass, rose gold, or black PVD metallic grooves running vertically or horizontally lend an understated, bespoke elegance without adding overwhelming visual bulk.

C. Double Rebated Safety Doors with Stainless Steel Wire Mesh (Jali)

A classic, practical Delhi favorite. In many builder floors and colonies, homeowners prefer a double-door system: an ornamental outer door with high-tensile 304-grade black stainless steel insect mesh (providing continuous cross-ventilation and security during evenings), paired with a solid inner door that locks securely at night.

D. Ultra-Slim Fluted Glass & Black Aluminium French Doors

While primarily used for indoor study partitions, balcony entries, and dining transitions, modern penthouse foyers increasingly incorporate black powder-coated aluminium frames with 10mm acoustic fluted (reeded) toughened glass. They diffuse natural sunlight while maintaining complete visual privacy from elevator corridors.

E. Traditional Hand-Carved Solid Teak Doors with Brass Studs

For heritage bungalows, farmhouses, and pooja mandir entrances. Hand-carved by veteran artisans featuring floral motifs, traditional geometric rosettes, brass studs, and heavy brass door knockers (kundi), protected with deep natural oil or antique walnut PU polish.

4. The Unsung Hero: Why Your Chaukhat (Door Frame) Dictates Lifespan

A multi-lakh main door is only as good as the frame (chaukhat) holding it. In Delhi, cheap contractors often install unseasoned softwoods or hollow frames that shift after civil plastering, causing expensive door locks to misalign within months. At Delhi Interior, our door chaukhats adhere to strict standards:

  • Hardwood Selection: We fabricate chaukhats exclusively from seasoned Marandi (Champ Wood) or solid CP Teakwood with section sizes of 5" x 2.5" or 6" x 3" to easily support heavy solid shutters.
  • Acoustic Rubber Gaskets: Every frame features a precision rebated groove fitted with a continuous EPDM rubber buffer. This prevents noisy door-slamming and eliminates dust ingress from Delhi's dusty street air.
  • Matching Architraves: Rather than leaving rough plaster joints around the frame, we dress every aperture with 3-inch to 4-inch matching solid timber or veneered architrave moldings.

5. Surface Finishes: Italian PU Polish vs Melamine vs Laminate

The exterior surface of your main door faces intense UV sunlight, dust, and daily handling. Choosing the right finish determines how long your door retains its lustrous showroom look:

  • Italian Polyurethane (PU) Polish (Matte / High Gloss): The gold standard. Applied in 6 to 8 specialized spray coats in a dust-free booth. PU polish provides deep optical clarity, non-yellowing UV filters, and maximum resistance against scratches and water splashes. Available in open-grain (feeling the natural timber texture) or filled high-gloss mirror finish.
  • Melamine Polish: A traditional solvent-based polish. Economical, but prone to yellowing under direct sunlight over 3 to 5 years and brittle if exposed to rain.
  • Exterior-Grade Matt Laminate (1.0mm – 1.5mm): Highly recommended for utility entries and rental properties where zero maintenance is required. Resistant to surface impacts, grease, and pet claws.
  • Duco / Acrylic Solid Paint: Ideal for crisp European shaker-style or Victorian panel doors in sophisticated shades of Charcoal Grey, Deep Navy Blue, or Off-White.

6. Smart Hardware & Security Automation

Modern luxury doors are high-performance mechanical assemblies. Here are the hardware specifications we engineer into every Delhi Interior door:

  • Heavy-Duty Pivot Hinges: German and Italian engineered pivot sets (Hafele / Ozone) featuring integrated 360-degree adjustability, built-in soft-close hydraulic dampers, and a hold-open position at 90 degrees.
  • Concealed 3D Adjustable Hinges: For flush doors, these hinges are completely invisible when the door is closed, eliminating unsightly external knuckle barrels.
  • Biometric Digital Smart Locks: We machine precision factory cavities for top-tier digital locks (Yale, Godrej, Hafele, Philips). Features include 3D facial recognition, 0.3s fingerprint scanning, scramble PIN codes, RFID card entry, and Wi-Fi mobile notifications with video doorbell camera feeds.
  • Automatic Drop-Down Bottom Seals: Concealed inside the bottom edge of the door, a mechanical rubber seal drops down tightly against the floor when the door closes, blocking drafts, insects, and corridor noise.

8. Vastu Shastra Guidelines for Main Entrance Doors

In Delhi NCR, over 80% of our residential clients request strict compliance with Vastu Shastra principles for their main entrance. Here are five golden rules we incorporate into our door layouts:

  • Auspicious Placement: North, North-East, and East entrances are considered most auspicious for wealth and positive energy. For South or West entrances, specific metal and wood remedies (such as adding brass inlay trims) balance planetary influences.
  • Opening Direction: The main door should always open inward in a smooth clockwise direction, welcoming positive cosmic energy into the foyer.
  • Elevation & Threshold (Dehleez): A raised solid wood or marble threshold prevents positive domestic energy from flowing outwards and keeps ground dust away.
  • Superior Scale: The main door should always be noticeably larger, taller, and more substantial than any interior room door in the house.
  • No Creaking Sounds: Squeaking or dragging hinges are believed to cause family disputes and financial friction. We ensure precision bearing lubrication for silent operation.

9. How Delhi Interior Custom-Fabricates Doors in South Delhi

Unlike interior aggregators who merely resell prefabricated catalog doors, Delhi Interior operates its own dedicated woodworking studio and carpentry team right here at Mohammadpur, South Delhi 110066. Our delivery process ensures zero compromises:

  1. Aperture Laser Calibration: Our senior site engineers measure your masonry openings using digital laser meters to check diagonal squareness, floor plumb, and swing arcs.
  2. Timber Kiln Seasoning & Hand Selection: We inspect every wood plank for grain consistency, knots, and moisture content before cutting begins.
  3. Mortise-and-Tenon Framework: Frames and cores are constructed using traditional interlocking joinery reinforced with marine-grade adhesives.
  4. Controlled Spray Polishing: Polishing and staining occur in our South Delhi facility to eliminate airborne dust specks and deliver an immaculate automotive-smooth finish.
  5. One-Day Professional Site Installation: The finished door arrives pre-fitted with lock cavities and is hung cleanly by our master carpenters in just a few hours.
