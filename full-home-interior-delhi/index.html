Engineering Excellence

One Team. Zero Subcontracting. Total Peace of Mind.

Coordinating separate carpenters, civil masons, painters, electricians, and plumbers is exhausting and inevitably leads to delays and finger-pointing. At Delhi Interior, our turnkey service places your entire home under one dedicated project manager and an elite in-house team.

From structural civil redesign and Italian marble flooring to bespoke modular kitchens, custom wardrobes, false ceilings, and ambient lighting, we manage every single stage. You receive daily photo updates and move into your dream home on the exact date agreed upon.

Client FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about materials, timelines, craftsmanship standards, and payment terms.

A standard 3BHK turnkey transformation takes 60 to 75 days. We provide a milestone-linked calendar before commencement and adhere strictly to it.
For full home civil, electrical, and flooring work, we strongly recommend vacating the property so our teams can work without noise and dust constraints to finish faster.
Our core turnkey packages cover all fixed architectural woodwork, civil, electrical, plumbing, ceiling, and painting. Soft furnishings, appliances, and loose sofas can be curated on request.
We operate on transparent milestone stages: booking advance, 3D design sign-off, workshop production commencement, on-site framework completion, and final handover.
We conduct detailed multi-point quality audits across all civil, electrical, plumbing, and carpentry installations before handover, accompanied by original manufacturer documentation for certified hardware and materials.
