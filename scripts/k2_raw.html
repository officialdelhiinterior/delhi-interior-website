Story of K2India

K2India is a New Delhi–based multi-disciplinary design and architecture firm founded by Sunita Kohli (Padma Shri, 1992) and her daughter, architect Kohelika Kohli.

Bringing together architecture, interior design, interior styling, landscaping, and bespoke furniture under one vision, K2India is known for its refined, contextual, and highly crafted approach to design.

From private homes to heritage restorations, and across continents from India to Egypt and England, K2India’s projects reflect a commitment to timeless aesthetics, cultural sensitivity, and exceptional craftsmanship.
