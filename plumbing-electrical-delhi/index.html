Engineering Excellence

The Hidden Arteries of a Flawless Home.

The most expensive interior surfaces are only as good as the hidden civil, electrical, and plumbing infrastructure behind them. A single leaky pipe joint inside a wall can ruin hundreds of thousands of rupees worth of custom woodwork and Italian marble.

At Delhi Interior, our licensed in-house master electricians and plumbers manage complete MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) redesigns. We test every water supply line under high hydraulic pressure for 48 hours and megger-test all electrical circuits before closing any walls.
